An informal seminar for learning and discussing [Gestalten](https://people.mpim-bonn.mpg.de/scholze/Gestalten.pdf), with an emphasis on the categorical foundations, examples, and connections to various parts of mathematics.

[Back to seminars](/seminars/)

## Signup

Please sign up for talks using the shared Google Sheet. Talks may range from 50 to 100 minutes, depending on the speaker, to allow for both completeness and discussion.
